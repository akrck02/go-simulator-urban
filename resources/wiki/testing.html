🧪 Testing

Different kinds of places

The plugin is tested on six synthetic places that grow into very different towns, and on every one it checks what must hold anywhere: nothing built in water or on obstacles, no plots on top of each other, nothing outside the window, the floor space the people need and a settled area close to what towns of that size covered.

Place Settings Result

River market town (6,000) river, wall at 3,000, 0.7 compact core with back streets, across the bridge
Hilltop town (2,500) hill 140 m high, square houses rings along the contour lines

Valley street village (1,500) steep valley sides, river a street village between the slopes
Large plain city (80,000) three roads, two centres, 0.5 dense core, two walls, ring road and belt, suburbs

Everything else

go test ./...
URBAN_RULES=path/to/rules.yaml URBAN_UNTIL=20 go test -run RulesFile -cpuprofile cpu.out ./urban

The second command runs a rules file inside the test process until a year, with a CPU profile for go tool pprof. Other tests cover the street network, frontages, densification, walls rising in phases or by the calendar, colours from the scenario, and a town grown on the world’s elevation field matching the same town grown on the relief file, entity by entity.
